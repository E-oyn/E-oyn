# Ege Oyan

Economist and financial analyst based in Hamburg, Germany.

My background combines macroeconomics and applied econometrics with practical experience in project finance, investment analysis, valuation and operational controlling. I use Python to structure financial data, analyse economic and market developments, build forecasting workflows and connect quantitative results with their underlying business context.

I am currently exploring workflows that combine company reports, financial data and narrative disclosures into structured analysis.

## Analytical focus

- Financial modelling, valuation and investment analysis
- Project finance, cash-flow analysis and operational controlling
- Applied econometrics and time-series analysis
- Financial-data preparation and structured-text extraction
